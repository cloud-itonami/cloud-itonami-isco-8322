# Business Model: Independent Car & Van Driving Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-8322`
- ISCO-08: `8322`
- Occupation: Car, Taxi and Van Drivers
- Social impact: local-jobs, road-safety, transport-access

## Customer

- passengers
- local businesses
- delivery clients

## Offer

- trip acceptance and routing
- pre-trip inspection
- duty-hours logging
- reporting

## Revenue

- per-trip fee
- monthly driving-platform fee
- reporting package

## Trust Controls

- no trip acceptance beyond duty-hours limit without governor gate
- high-risk trip acceptance always requires human sign-off
- trip and duty-hours records are auditable, not editable
